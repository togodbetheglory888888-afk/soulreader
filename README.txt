Only age is requested. The app does not ask for a birthday, name, email, phone number, login, or account. Answers remain in the page session and are used locally to calculate the result.

## Important spiritual disclaimer
Soul Age and reincarnation-cycle results are symbolic, entertainment/self-reflection concepts. They are not scientifically validated measurements and do not establish the existence, number, or timing of past lives. Horoscope-style language is intentionally presented as a reflective reading rather than a supernatural fact.
